# Vision

Fuuna is a challenge marketplace that connects sponsors with people who complete real-world or online challenges in exchange for rewards.

The platform consists of three major user roles:

- User
- Sponsor
- Platform Administrator

Our goal is to build a secure, scalable, and user-friendly platform that works on Android and iOS.
